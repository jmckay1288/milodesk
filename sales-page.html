Your part

What we need from you — in detail

We build and run everything. Your part is small, and here is all of it, with no surprises later:

1. One short intake

About 15 minutes, once: your business name, services, hours, service area, the prices (if any) you're comfortable having quoted, and where you want leads sent — your phone, your email, or both. This is what your chat, site, and follow-ups are built from.

2. Approve your setup

Before anything goes live, we send you your website text, the answers your chat will give, and your follow-up wording to look over. Nothing is published in your name, and no answer goes out to your customers, without your okay.

3. Access to your Google profile

Add us as a manager on your Google Business Profile — we send a one-minute how-to. If you don't have a profile yet, we create one with you. This is what lets us fix your listing and run your review requests.

4. Google's verification video

If Google asks to verify your business, this step is yours alone — Google requires the owner. It's a short video filmed on your phone showing your work vehicle, tools, equipment, or signage. We give you a checklist of exactly what to film, and we handle everything after Google approves it.

5. Your domain choice

Your included website can live free on an address under ours, or on your own domain name. Your own domain costs about $12 a year and is registered in your name — you'll complete that purchase yourself, since it's your account and your payment. Either way, we connect it and put it live.

6. Call forwarding (phone add-on)

If you add phone answering, you turn on call forwarding from your existing number to your AI number — we send step-by-step instructions for your carrier, and it takes a few minutes. You also set your emergency rule: urgent calls transfer straight to your cell, or come to you as an urgent message.

7. One tap on review replies

We draft a reply to every Google review for you. Your part is one tap to approve it — or, if you'd rather never think about it, tell us once and we'll post the replies for you.

8. Tell us when things change

New hours, a service added or dropped, a price change, a new phone number — one quick message to us keeps your website, chat, and Google listing accurate. That's the whole ongoing job.

And two honest notes: the pilot is free; if you stay on afterwards, your payment card goes on file for the monthly plan, and you can cancel anytime. And we catch, chase, and hand you every lead fast — calling the customer back and doing the work itself stays with you.

Questions

What owners usually ask

I don't have a website. Can I still use this?

Yes. The chat can run on a simple page we set up for you, and the lead tracker, follow-ups, and review requests all work whether or not you have a site today. If you do have a website, the chat is added to it.

What happens after the free 14 days?

You'll see every lead the pilot caught. If you want to keep going, it's $79/month flat — no setup fee and no contract. If not, it switches off and you owe nothing. The pilot is the proof, not a trap.

Will it quote prices or promise times to my customers?

No. The assistant never quotes exact prices and never promises arrival times. It answers basic questions you've approved — hours, service area, what you offer — and captures the customer's details and the job so you can quote it yourself.

How do I actually get my leads?

Two ways, instantly: a short summary in your messages the moment a lead comes in, and the lead tracker, where every lead lands in one list with its status. Phone-answering calls also keep a recording and transcript.

Do I have to change my phone number or anything else?

No. Your number, your website, and your tools stay the same. For phone answering, we set your line to forward when you're busy or after hours — a one-time setting on your existing number.

What if I don't have a website?

Then we build you one — a simple one-page website is included free with the package, and your chat and lead intake go on it. For the address, you can use a free one under ours (like yourbusiness.milodesk.com), or your own domain name: about $12 a year, registered in your name and yours to keep, and we connect it for you.

Is a robot going to sound bad to my customers?

Try the demo above — that's the standard. The assistant is set up with your business name, your services, and your rules, and anything it can't handle becomes a message to you instead of a dead end. Customers always reach a person: you, with their details already in hand.
